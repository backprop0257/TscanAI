How it works

  1. ResizeYour photo is shrunk to at most 1280 px in the browser before upload.
  2. Background removal (V3)The leaf is cut out and cropped; soil, stones, mulch, grass and shadows are removed while diseased spots are kept — the same method the models were trained with.
  3. Tomato-leaf checkPhoto quality, then leaf shape, toothed edge, veins, colour and texture, then a deep check against the tomato training data (OOD-gate CNN, Mahalanobis and energy scores). Accept threshold {{ "%.2f"|format(cfg.ood_threshold) }}{% if not cfg.gate_calibrated %} (deep stage not calibrated yet){% endif %}.
  4. PredictionAny model in models/ can be used; each gets the crop at its own input size and scaling. It picks one of its classes ({{ class_names|length }} tomato classes for the notebook models); confidence is calibrated with the model’s own fitted temperature when one exists (notebook Cell 8.2).
  5. Grad-CAMA heatmap on the model’s last feature map (the native 14×14 lesion map for v21 models; the ViT token grid or a gradient saliency map for models without one). The Leaf-Focus Score says how much of the attention is on the leaf, the Lesion-Focus Score how much is on diseased tissue.
  6. Decision & adviceUnder {{ "%.0f"|format(cfg.conf_threshold * 100) }}% confidence it says “Not sure” instead of guessing, then shows management advice.
